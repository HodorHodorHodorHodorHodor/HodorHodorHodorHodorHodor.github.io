Bento Pack Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

The short version

  • Bento Pack has no accounts, and we never ask for your name, email, photos, contacts or location.
  • Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. We set every ad request as child-directed, non-personalized and limited to G-rated content. We don't track you across other apps.
  • An optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is handled by Apple (or Google Play). RevenueCat helps us confirm it. We never see your payment details.

Who we are

Bento Pack ("the app") is made by Michael Kim ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy: waypointcustomersupport@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your level progress, stars, daily streak, settings (sound, vibration, kid mode) and whether you bought Remove Ads. This stays on your device and isn't sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Bento Pack free, it shows ads through Google AdMob. Because people of all ages, including children, play the app, we configure AdMob to:

  • treat every ad request as child-directed (under COPPA) and as for users under the age of consent;
  • request non-personalized ads only (no ads based on your interests or activity in other apps);
  • allow only ads rated G (suitable for all audiences).

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information. This can include your IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), device type, operating system, app version, and whether an ad was shown or tapped. We don't use your device's advertising identifier to track you, and the app doesn't ask for tracking permission. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message may ask for your choices before ads load.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Apple, Google Play and RevenueCat)

Remove Ads is an optional one-time purchase, protected in the app by a "grown-ups only" check. Payment is handled entirely by Apple's App Store (or Google Play). We never receive your name, card number or billing address.

We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore them on a new device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, the purchase receipt and transaction details, and basic device information (such as operating system and app version). See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Bento Pack is made for everyone, including children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app doesn't let children share personal information, chat, or make purchases without passing a grown-up check. Ads are configured as child-directed, as described above. A parent or guardian with any question or request can contact us at waypointcustomersupport@gmail.com, and we'll help.

Your choices

  • Remove ads any time with the one-time purchase in Settings.
  • Reset your progress in Settings, or delete the app to remove everything stored on the device.
  • Where Google's consent message applies, you can review your ad choices in Settings when that option is shown.
  • You can contact us to ask about or request deletion of any information. We'll forward requests to Google or RevenueCat where they hold the data.

Data sharing and selling

We don't sell personal information. We don't share information with anyone except the service providers above (Google AdMob, RevenueCat, Apple/Google Play), and only to run the app, show ads and process purchases, or when the law requires it.

Security and retention

We keep as little information as possible. What's stored on your device stays until you reset it or delete the app. Our service providers keep data according to their own policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
